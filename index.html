About REAA

A scientific association
built on rigour & openness

REAA is an independent scientific association based in Tunisia, dedicated to producing high-quality, open research in analytics and artificial intelligence. We bring together researchers, engineers, and domain experts to tackle complex problems through collaborative inquiry.

Our work spans fundamental research — statistical learning theory, causal inference, knowledge representation — and applied studies in health informatics, smart agriculture, urban systems, and public policy analytics.

Research Areas

Where we focus

Our research portfolio is organised around four interconnected themes, each addressed through dedicated working groups.

Core Programme

Machine Learning & Statistical Inference

Developing robust learning algorithms, Bayesian methods, and uncertainty quantification frameworks suited to low-resource environments.

  • Deep learning generalisation
  • Probabilistic graphical models
  • Federated & privacy-preserving ML
Applied

Health & Biomedical Analytics

AI-assisted diagnostics, electronic health record analysis, and epidemiological modelling for under-resourced healthcare systems.

Applied

Smart Agriculture & Environment

Remote sensing, precision farming data pipelines, and climate-aware predictive models for North African agricultural contexts.

Cross-cutting

Responsible AI & Policy

Algorithmic fairness, explainability methods, governance frameworks, and science-policy interface for AI regulation.
